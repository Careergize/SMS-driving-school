What are the driving course fees?

LMV car training is ₹11,000, MCWG geared motorcycle training is ₹8,000 and MCWOG scooter training is ₹6,000. LL Fee & Registration is listed separately at ₹2,000.

Is the ₹2,000 LL fee included in training?

The website lists LL Fee & Registration separately. Use the fee estimator to add it to your selected course and confirm the complete fee breakdown with the school.

Can I join as a complete beginner?

Yes. Contact us with the vehicle you want to learn and your experience level so we can discuss the right starting point.

How many lessons will I receive?

Please confirm the number and duration of lessons with the school before enrolling. The listed rates do not specify a fixed lesson count.

Can I choose my lesson timings?

Share your preferred days and times when you enquire. Our team will confirm available slots; submitting an enquiry does not reserve a slot.

What documents do I need?

Contact the school for the document checklist for your chosen licence category. You can ask about identity, address and age documentation and the application steps.

Where is SMS Motor Driving School?

Find our Vanchiyoor, Kulathoor and Peroorkada addresses in the branch section. For Manacaud, contact the main office to confirm the full address and direct number before visiting.

Do you offer pickup, automatic or refresher lessons?

Ask the school about current availability, service areas and pricing for these options before booking.
